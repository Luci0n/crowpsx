Before you build

A few useful answers.

Does CrowPSX work with URP?

Yes. CrowPSX supports Built-in and URP with both SubShaders in the same shader assets. URP outlines and depth-volume decals require their CrowPSX renderer features. Grab Distortion requires Opaque Texture.

Is this a full-screen PS1 filter?

CrowPSX is a shader and material-authoring suite. Its snapping and affine controls work on geometry and materials. Camera resolution, full-screen dithering, and CRT presentation are separate choices; do not assume the suite supplies a complete camera post-processing stack.

What Unity version do I need?

Use Unity 2022.3 LTS or newer. The package documentation lists testing on 2022.3, 6.0, 6.3, and 6.6. The current Asset Store compatibility table explicitly lists 2022.3.57f1 with Built-in and URP. HDRP shaders are not included. Check your exact Unity and URP combination before committing a production project.

Can I use it in VR?

The package documents stereo-stable snapping and separate VR snap and affine strengths, with Multi-Pass, Single Pass Instanced, and Multiview support depending on the pipeline. See the setup notes for mode-specific constraints.
